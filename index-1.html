Batang Lansangan is a community-help project supporting children, families, and other people experiencing homelessness or hardship. Every act of kindness can help provide food and other basic needs.

Our mission

We aim to offer practical, compassionate help to children without stable homes, families living on the streets, and anyone in need. With community support, we hope to provide food and basic necessities and share updates about the help given.
